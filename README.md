## 🌟 Featured Engineering Projects

### 1. 🌾 [Agro AI-suite: AI-Powered Smart Farming Platform](https://github.com/dhanarajesuru-png)
* **Problem**: Remote farming communities experience severe crop losses due to delayed disease diagnosis and lack of immediate agronomy advisory.
* **Solution**: End-to-end platform pairing a fine-tuned PyTorch vision classifier with an asynchronous Google Gemini RAG agronomy advisory engine.
* **Key Features**:
  * **94.2% Diagnostic Accuracy** across 14 plant disease classes with **<48ms inference latency**.
  * Asynchronous FastAPI backend with SQLite Write-Ahead Logging (WAL) caching.
  * Offline-first Progressive Web App (PWA) with live leaf inference simulation.
* **Tech Stack**: `PyTorch`, `Google Gemini API`, `FastAPI (Async)`, `SQLite WAL`, `Docker`, `PWA`

---

### 2. 🧠 [Aura Mind: AI Clinical Mental Health Companion](https://github.com/dhanarajesuru-png)
* **Problem**: Mental health chatbots risk critical safety containment failures during acute crisis moments and expose sensitive user emotional records.
* **Solution**: Privacy-first clinical wellness companion engineered with real-time crisis containment guardrails, offline CBT/DBT exercise retrieval, and client-side AES-256 encryption.
* **Key Features**:
  * Dual-layer sentiment guardrails intercepting self-harm intents with emergency hotline routing.
  * Offline TF-IDF therapeutic exercise retrieval without network dependencies.
  * Client-side AES-256 encryption for mood logs, Web Speech voice journaling, and PDF clinical export.
* **Tech Stack**: `React`, `Node.js / Express`, `Google Gemini API`, `TF-IDF RAG`, `AES-256`, `SQLite`
